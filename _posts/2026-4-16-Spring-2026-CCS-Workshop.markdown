## What We Actually Work On

The workshop centered on technologies used in the field, not just theory — CO₂ storage
(**CCS**), rock-structure analysis (**Digital Rock**), fluid-flow **simulation**, and
data-driven **AI/ML** modeling. In short: a fusion of geology, fluids, data, and AI.

## Researcher Presentations

Lab members presented projects underway right now — Micro-CT-based rock structure
analysis (Digital Rock), pore-scale flow with the Lattice Boltzmann Method (LBM),
diffusion-model-based seismic interpretation, and CCS storage operation efficiency.
These are active research projects, not textbook exercises.

<div class="data-flow"></div>

## Where We're Headed

Looking ahead, the lab is connecting experiments, simulation, and AI — building Digital
Twin storage-prediction technology and large-scale modeling designed for real industry
use.

> **The goal of our research is not "a paper" but "field application."**
