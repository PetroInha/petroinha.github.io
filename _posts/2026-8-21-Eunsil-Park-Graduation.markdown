## Selected Work

Eunsil leaves behind a research record any graduate would be proud of — first-author
papers in two leading journals and a poster at Europe's largest geosciences meeting.

<ul class="pub-list">

  <li>
    <span class="pub-venue">IEEE TGRS · 2026</span><br>

    Characterization Using ES-MDA. <em>IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote
    Sensing</em>.
  </li>

  <li>
    <span class="pub-venue">Int. J. Greenhouse Gas Control · 2025</span><br>

    <em>International Journal of Greenhouse Gas Control</em>, 147, 104461.

  <li>
    <span class="pub-venue">EGU General Assembly · 2026 · Poster</span><br>

She also contributed to collaborative work on SinGAN–ES-MDA history matching,
seismic-driven geostatistical ensemble modeling of the West Sea CO₂ storage
reservoir, and generative-AI digital rock augmentation — a reminder of how much a
single dedicated student can lift an entire lab.
