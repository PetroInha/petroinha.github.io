  <div class="grad-fact">

      full field scale.
    </p>
  </div>

Eunsil leaves behind a research record any graduate would be proud of — first-author
papers in two leading journals and a poster at Europe's largest geosciences meeting.

  <li>

seismic-driven geostatistical ensemble modeling of the West Sea CO₂ storage
reservoir, and generative-AI digital rock augmentation — a reminder of how much a
single dedicated student can lift an entire lab.

<div class="grad-section-head">
<h2>이영진 · Yongjin Lee</h2>
<span class="grad-badge phd">Continuing to Ph.D.</span>
</div>

<div class="grad-profile">

  <div class="grad-fact">
    <div class="grad-fact-label">Research Focus</div>
    <div class="grad-fact-value">SAGD · Reinforcement Learning · ES-SAGD</div>
    <p class="grad-fact-note">
      Treating thermal recovery as a sequential decision problem — using
      reinforcement learning to search for steam and solvent injection strategies
      that a fixed operating rule would never find.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="grad-fact next-phd">
    <div class="grad-fact-label">Next Chapter</div>
    <div class="grad-fact-value">Ph.D. Program · Prof. Hyundon Shin</div>
    <p class="grad-fact-note">
      Staying with CURE to take his master's work further, deepening the link between
      experimental ES-SAGD evidence and intelligent operation control for
      lower-carbon oil sands development.
    </p>
  </div>

</div>

<div class="highlight-box">
<p>
🏅 <strong>A standout contribution to the ES-SAGD experiment.</strong> Yongjin played
a central role in our expansion-solvent SAGD experimental campaign, where laboratory
work is unforgiving and every result is hard won. What he achieved there gave the
group experimental grounding for the solvent co-injection behavior we simulate —
exactly the kind of bridge between bench and simulator that heavy-oil research needs.
</p>
</div>

Pairing hands-on experimental work with reinforcement learning is an unusual
combination, and a powerful one: the experiments tell you what the physics really
does, while the learning agent explores an operating space far larger than anything
that can be tested by hand. We are delighted he is continuing that line of work
with us.
