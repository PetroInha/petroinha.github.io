## Oil Sands: Advanced Reservoir Simulation

The opening session featured Prof. Hyundon Shin's talk on **Oil Sands Production
Technology and Simulation**. Oil sands — a complex mix of oil, sand, and clay — require
high-temperature, high-pressure steam injection (SAGD) to reduce viscosity and improve
recovery.

<div class="post-quote">"Accurate prediction of how steam spreads and how the formation responds determines production performance — simulation precision decides everything."</div>

With high initial CAPEX, prediction failure carries enormous risk, making history
matching and accurate petrophysical modeling essential.

<div class="data-flow"></div>

## CCS, Lithium, Hydrogen — The Full Spectrum

Later sessions spanned the breadth of next-generation energy technology: SLB's thermal
simulation advances; CCS database construction and cross-border M&amp;A trends from KIGAM
and KNOC; Direct Lithium Extraction (DLE) from brine; and hydrogen and unconventional
resource development.

## Message from Prof. Hyundon Shin

<div class="post-quote">"Even in the era of carbon neutrality, unconventional resource development remains critical for energy security and key material procurement."</div>

The workshop was a powerful reminder that the scope of energy engineering is broad, the
challenges are real, and the opportunities for students and researchers are greater than
ever.
