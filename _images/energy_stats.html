How this is built (workflow)

  1. Collect — daily bars are pulled from Yahoo Finance (yfinance) for tradable contracts and from the St. Louis Fed's public FRED CSV endpoint for rates, the trade-weighted dollar and the weekly EIA fundamentals. Each source is fetched independently; one that fails is dropped and flagged below rather than aborting the run.
  2. Align — every series is resampled onto a business-day grid and forward-filled (max 10 days) so weekly and monthly statistics can sit alongside daily prices in one panel.
  3. Engineer features — for each indicator: 1/3/5/10-day changes normalised by 60-day realised volatility, plus a 60-day level z-score. Differences rather than log returns are used so that yield and real-rate series (which can be zero or negative) stay well defined.
  4. Fit — a 500-tree RandomForestRegressor maps today's full cross-market state onto the log price change 5 trading days ahead (~1 week). The model trains on 4 years of history, not on the one month displayed — a month of daily bars is far too little to fit a forest on.
  5. Quantify uncertainty — a 5-fold TimeSeriesSplit walk-forward produces out-of-sample residuals. The predictive distribution is the point forecast plus the empirical residual distribution; P10/P50/P90 are its 10th/50th/90th percentiles, converted back to price. This is deliberately wider and more honest than the spread of the trees alone, which only captures model variance and badly understates real forecast error.
  6. Render — Plotly subplots, oil and gas given the top two rows at roughly 2.5× the height of the context panels.

Caveats. Dubai crude has no free daily feed; the monthly FRED benchmark is forward-filled and shown dotted for reference only. EUA carbon is represented by the KRBN ETF as a proxy, not the ICE EUA futures settlement. Weekly EIA and monthly IP series are step-plotted at their release dates. The P10/P50/P90 band is a statistical description of one model's historical error, not a market view or trading advice — it assumes the next week resembles the training period and will be wrong whenever it does not.
